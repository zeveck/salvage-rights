# Brief: Salvage Rights

**One sentence:** Drive a scrappy remote-controlled robot through a ladder of arena fights, and after every win rip a part off the loser and bolt it onto your own machine until you are the champion.

**Feeling:** Crunchy, loud, gleeful mechanical violence with a garage-tinkerer's pride: "I built this monster out of everyone I beat."

## What they asked for
- [ ] Start with a simple robot
- [ ] Battle other robots in a battle arena, kinda like Battle Bots
- [ ] More elaborate robots (than real-life battle bots)
- [ ] When you win, take a part from the other robot, which upgrades you
- [ ] A series of battles, getting stronger
- [ ] Move through the ranks to be the battle champion
- [ ] Between battles a "garage" *or something* (loose) to change out parts and buffs
- [ ] Sell/buy in the garage
- [ ] A variety of arenas
- [ ] "Probably" 3D (loose, but `--3d` makes it firm)
- [ ] You remote control during the fight
- [ ] Cool weapons, powers, etc.
- [ ] Redesign and paint your robot

## Who, where, how long
Anyone who likes robots, car-combat or build-and-brawl games. Desktop (keyboard) and phone (landscape, touch). Fights are 1-2 minutes; the whole climb about 30-40 minutes, in sittings of any length.

## Style references
"Battle Bots" (named by the person) is a reference for the *sport*: a steel arena, arena hazards, spinners, flippers, hammers, sparks, a TV-show energy. Borrow: fixed "driver's booth" remote-control framing, knockouts, judges' decision when time runs out, hazards. Avoid copying: any of its robots, names, logos, branding, or arena look. All names and designs here are original.

## Settings and decisions on the person's behalf
- Web, desktop and phone, 3D, $25 budget, publish to GitHub Pages with no confirmation stop.
- Name: Salvage Rights.
- "Garage or something": a literal garage hub, with a parts bench, parts exchange (buy/sell) and paint booth.
- "Remote control": you are a driver in a booth. The default camera is the booth's view of the arena; a chase cam is optional; EMP hits show "link lost" static on your remote feed.
